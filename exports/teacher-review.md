## b1-01 · A rainy street festival

**B1 / 節日**

- **Maya:** The street festival starts at eleven, but the forecast says heavy rain after two.
- **Tom:** Then let’s arrive early. I mainly want to see the lion dance at half past eleven.
- **Maya:** Good idea. We can have lunch at the community hall if the outdoor tables are wet.
- **Tom:** I’ll bring a light raincoat. The organisers say umbrellas block people’s view.

1. What does Tom most want to see?
   - A. The lion dance / B. The food market / C. A fireworks show
   - **答案：A** — Tom 明確說他主要想看十一時半的舞獅。
2. Why will they arrive early?
   - A. To avoid an afternoon downpour / B. To help set up tables / C. To buy cheaper tickets
   - **答案：A** — 預報下午二時後有大雨，所以他們決定早到。
3. Which item is more suitable than an umbrella?
   - A. A light raincoat / B. A picnic blanket / C. A large hat
   - **答案：A** — 主辦方表示雨傘會阻礙視線，因此 Tom 會帶輕便雨衣。
