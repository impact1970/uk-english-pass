# UK English Pass — preliminary trade mark search

Search date: 6 November 2026 (Europe/London)

## Scope and conclusion

This is a preliminary knockout search, not a legal clearance opinion. The exact
phrase `UK English Pass` produced no result in the EUIPO-operated TMview search
across its participating collections. The broader phrase `English Pass`
produced 50 results worldwide, including active marks connected with education,
software or online services in Brazil and Japan. The name therefore has an
**amber risk rating** for international commercial use: the added `UK` element
helps distinguish it, but `English Pass` is already used by third parties.

No paid app should launch globally on this report alone. Obtain a professional
clearance search for the intended territories and Nice classes before filing or
substantial marketing spend.

## Recommended filing classes

Obtain professional advice on the exact specification. The likely starting
classes are:

- Class 9: downloadable mobile applications and downloadable educational media;
- Class 41: education, language learning and provision of online exercises; and
- Class 42 only if the commercial service includes non-downloadable software or
  software-as-a-service functions beyond the installed paid app.
