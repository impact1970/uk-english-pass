# Content licensing map

UK English Pass uses two distribution licences so the public learning resource
and a paid mobile application can coexist without ambiguous notices.

| Distribution | Licence | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public web edition | CC BY-NC 4.0 | Original scripts, questions and Hong Kong Traditional Chinese explanations in the public release |
| Paid commercial app edition | UK English Pass Commercial App Licence 1.0 | The paid app package, commercial-edition presentation and content as supplied in that edition |

The same rights holder may offer the same material under more than one licence.
Existing CC BY-NC 4.0 permissions are not revoked. Third-party components are
excluded from both content licences and retain the terms listed in
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and `THIRD_PARTY_PACKAGE_LICENSES.md`.

The legal seller name in the final Apple App Store and Google Play listings must
be checked before submission. If the seller is a person or registered company
rather than the UK English Pass trading name, update the copyright owner and
licensor identification in the app metadata and legal screen.
